Subject: Statistical-query learning and dimension complexity

Dear Professors Feldman, Kamath and Srebro,

I share a manuscript addressing Open Question 2 in your note.

A product-distribution rectangle bound of 1/rho yields one distribution-independent SQ learner.
It uses O(rho(log|H| + log(1/epsilon))) queries at tolerance Omega(epsilon/(rho log(1/epsilon))).
Incidence-flip classes separate this complexity from ordinary and both nondegenerate probabilistic dimensions.

The construction randomly flips grid-line incidences while preserving large monochromatic rectangles. The learner queries marginal masses and alternates between labeling regions and eliminating hypotheses.

The incidence construction and minimax ingredient are due to Hatami, Hatami, Pires, Tao and Zhao; the counting uses Warren's theorem. The manuscript includes proper learners, computational implementations, and a matching query-order lower bound for typical flips. VALG v2 proves a dimension bound under an additional polynomial mean-response-rank assumption; the comparison is stated separately. OQ1 remains open.
