\section*{Summary of results}
\begin{center}\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X p{.20\linewidth}p{.14\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Statement & Status & Reference\\\midrule
$\rect(H)\ge1/\rho$ gives $O(\rho(\log K+\log(1/\epsilon)))$ queries and tolerance $\Omega(\epsilon/(\rho\log(1/\epsilon)))$. & Unconditional & \cref{thm:main}\\
Incidence flips have dimension $\Omega(N/\log M)$ and constant inverse rectangle ratio. & Unconditional & \cref{thm:construction}\\
Exact and averaged probabilistic dimension remain $\Omega(N/\log^2M)$ and $\Omega_\eta(N/\log M)$. & Unconditional & \cref{thm:prob,lem:exact-prob}\\
All three proposed SQ-to-dimension implications fail. & Unconditional & \cref{cor:negative-answer}\\
Typical flips require $\Theta_\epsilon(n)$ queries; the leading proper lower bound is $[n-O_\epsilon(1)]/\log_2\lceil1/\tau\rceil$. & Unconditional & \cref{thm:query-lower}\\
Caps give deterministic table-polynomial implementations; Proper RECTIFY returns a member. & Unconditional & \cref{thm:caps,thm:proper}\\
Succinct evaluation gives a deterministic improper $O(n^2)$-query learner; regular stars give proper learning. & Unconditional & \cref{thm:fast}\\
For each fixed $c$, almost every polynomial-length key has dimension at least $n^c/(30\log_2 n)$. & PRF assumption & \cref{thm:prf}\\
Almost every key has a key-known proper polynomial-time learner. & PRF assumption & \cref{cor:fastprf}\\
Polynomial advice gives almost-every-advice high dimension and proper $O_\epsilon(\log n)$-query learning. & Unconditional & \cref{prop:advice}\\
Engineered polynomial-size full-batch gradient models learn every key; high dimension holds for almost every key. & High dimension: PRF & \cref{cor:engineered}\\
Bounded product-average communication implies SQ learnability; halfspaces show non-necessity. & Unconditional & \cref{thm:communication,thm:halfspaces}\\
Hidden-table sample bounds require a table-independent learner. & Unconditional & \cref{prop:unseen}\\
Benign online SGD, efficient deterministic selection, succinct $O(n)$ queries, and the optimal $(m,\tau)$ tradeoff. & Open & Section~\ref{sec:open}\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
The qualifier ``typical'' means outside the explicit exceptional probabilities in \cref{thm:construction,thm:prob,thm:query-lower}. The polynomial-time key statements fix $c$ before the asymptotic limit. Exact constants and source-to-proof checks appear in the appendices and accompanying verification ledger.
