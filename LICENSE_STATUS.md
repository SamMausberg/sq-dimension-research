the all-rights-reserved notice in [LICENSE](LICENSE), credited to Samuel Mausberg.
No open-source, Creative Commons, or arXiv submission license has been selected.
Public release requires a separate decision by the author.

[CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) records the manuscript's title and author and supplies
GitHub's citation metadata. It does not confer reuse rights or reserve ownership
of mathematical ideas. Neither a notice nor a citation file guarantees against
plagiarism. Git history and preserved source checksums document this archive's
provenance; they are not independent proof of first discovery.
