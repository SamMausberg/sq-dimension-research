\paragraph{Oracle and distribution access.}
The learners receive only the entry evaluator and SQ answers; the simulator alone knows the chosen target and rational distribution. Every query records its support mask, exact expectation, returned rational answer and public control state. The adversarial policy chooses a tolerance endpoint using the complete public transcript and the current query. For threshold decisions it chooses an endpoint that favors premature stopping, ambiguous median comparisons, a high-error candidate acceptance, or continued search when available, according to the explicit priority rules in the scripts. The cap marginal policy cannot inspect a private row side or color not revealed by the query. The policy is the explicit public-history endpoint adversary implemented in the scripts. Exact-oracle runs are reported separately in the raw outputs.

Structured distributions include uniform template points, actual negative points, one heavy negative point with a small tail, and mixed positive/negative supports. The searches use random restarts followed by coordinate changes of rational weights, with support at most 20. The order-median sweep uses three restarts and four mutations; the cap sweep uses two restarts and three mutations. Reporting uses fresh learner seeds. Every recorded error is recomputed against the actual target, and every recorded proper output is checked for class membership.

\paragraph{Succinct order-median learner.}
The script \texttt{seeded\_fast.py} executes the proper algorithm of Theorem~\ref{thm:fast} for $N=2,\ldots,8,16,32$. It does not construct a truth table or enumerate all domain points inside the learner. The simulator represents $D$ by its finite support. The stated accuracy is $\epsilon=1/10$, and the exact-rational experiments use effective tolerance $\zeta=\epsilon/4096=1/40960$. The finite-bit theorem requests raw tolerance $\zeta/2$ to reserve the other half for response rounding; these runs have no additional rounding error.

\paragraph{Keyed Proper RECTIFY.}
The script \texttt{run\_keyed\_caps.py} runs the proper cap learner on the same keyed family for $N=2,\ldots,8$, using only the public-history endpoint oracle. It uses outer accuracy $1/10$, rectangle-phase accuracy $1/40$, $\rho=2^{20}$, and tolerance $1/23226329280$. A numerical radial-isotropic optimizer proposes a finite catalog of directions and rectangles. Before that catalog is used, the integer truth table verifies every rectangle and the exact pointwise coverage of the discrete mixture. Thus the implemented coverage guarantee does not rely on rounding a numerical covariance residual in the favorable direction.

The fresh exact checks cover 162 endpoint inequalities, 4,080 nonmonotone median paths, 1,000 prefix--suffix identities, 1,000 fixed-bit star samplers, 3,487 ambient-encoding comparisons, 5,000 response-grid cases, 17,476 halfspace color reductions and 720 unseen-label bounds.
