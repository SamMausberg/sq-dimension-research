Distribution-independent SQ learning does not imply low dimension complexity

Compile with stock TeX Live / pdfLaTeX, with shell escape disabled:
  pdflatex -no-shell-escape -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
  pdflatex -no-shell-escape -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
  pdflatex -no-shell-escape -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex

main.bbl is supplied. To regenerate it after modifying references.bib,
run bibtex main between the first and second pdfLaTeX passes.
No absolute paths, external image/font files, network calls or shell-escape
commands are needed. All figures are TikZ source.

Suggested categories: cs.LG primary; cs.CC, math.CO, stat.ML secondary.
The cover email, verification reports and experiments are separate supplements.
The archive is technically build-ready; source-verification limitations are
listed in the accompanying author checklist. No arXiv upload was performed.
