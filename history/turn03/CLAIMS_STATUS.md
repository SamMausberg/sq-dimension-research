## Executed but not universal optimality claims

The public-history endpoint oracle and distribution search are explicit adversaries, not global maximizers.
Numerical radial transforms are approximate; exact finite coverage is certified only for the recorded catalog states.
Product-distribution rectangle values are exact for those witnesses, not globally minimizing products.
Observed error and query maxima apply only to the 448 recorded runs.

## Unverified or open

Every Lean proof script remains uncompiled. The new main results have no completed Lean formalization.

No ordinary Gaussian-initialized bias-free ReLU online-SGD learning theorem or counterexample is proved.
No tight joint query/tolerance characterization follows from the projective-plane lower bound.
No general finite-time/expanded-circuit description repair or rectangle-type characterization of distribution-independent SQ complexity is proved.
